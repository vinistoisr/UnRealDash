# Chunk 12: analog dial, bar gauge and history graph (PLAN 4.6)

All ten criteria pass on this machine. All nine schema component types now have builders, and the
dial keeps its shape at both viewport heights with a control that has been watched failing.

The build found something the spec did not anticipate and that chunk 11 had shipped with: **a
document had no way to say what is in front of what.** The dial rendered underneath its own face
image, and no ordering the document could express changed it. That is fixed by adding an optional
`z_order` to the component schema, which is the only schema change either chunk has made.

## What the build found

### A document could not express stacking, and still cannot express it well

The dial drew underneath its own face. Four hypotheses were eliminated by measurement before the
cause was found, and the order matters because three of them were plausible:

1. **The slot geometry.** Ruled out: a needle drawn as a full-panel fill with no transform was also
   invisible, and with the face removed the real needle measured 191 by 191 at ratio 1.000000.
2. **The render transform.** Ruled out by the same test.
3. **Paint order from `UCanvasPanelSlot::ZOrder`.** Setting it from the delivered component index
   changed nothing.
4. **The image primitive being special.** Ruled out: a `shape` face occluded the needle too.

The cause is `BoundedParse.cpp:118-132`, which sorts the members of every object so that schema
diagnostics do not depend on the author's ordering. That is a sound decision, and JSON object member
order is not significant anyway. But `components` is an object, so stacking fell out as alphabetical
by component id: `dial` before `face`. Chunk 11 was unaffected only because `gauge_fill` sorts
before `speed`.

Adding optional `z_order` to the schema is the fix. Stacking is layout, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` says
the document is the only source of layout. The alternative, stacking by component id, is layout by
naming convention, which is the kind of shortcut that needs undoing later.

**This was live in chunk 11 as well.** Any two overlapping siblings there stacked alphabetically with
no way to say otherwise. Nothing in its gate fixture depended on it, so it shipped green.

### `scaling: stretch` does not stretch, and nothing depends on it

Criterion 4's first control set the root component's `scaling` to `stretch`, which
`BuildDocumentRoot` maps to `EStretch::Fill`, documented in the engine's `EStretch` enum as "Scales
the content non-uniformly filling the entire space of the area".

Measured at 1280x660, the face came out 367 by 366, a ratio of 1.0027: still square, and scaled
uniformly by about 0.61 rather than 1.0 by 0.9167. So the control PASSED, which would have left
criteria 2 and 3 proving nothing, and the gate reported that as a failure.

The control was rebuilt on `aspect_policy`, which was measured to work. **Why `EStretch::Fill`
behaves uniformly here is not resolved**, and is recorded as open rather than guessed at. Nothing in
Stage 0 depends on `scaling: stretch`; the only value used anywhere is `uniform`. It is worth
settling before PLAN 5.x, where a head unit at a different aspect ratio makes the document-to-viewport
mapping matter.

### The validator protects the dial but not its face

`SemanticPass.cpp:273` rejects `stretch` on an `analog_dial` and on a circular `bar_gauge`. It says
nothing about the `image` that forms a dial's face. Criterion 4a is exactly such a document: it
stretches a dial face to a ratio of 1.5 and loads cleanly.

A stretched face ruins a dial as thoroughly as a stretched needle would. Not fixed here, because
tightening it means deciding which images are gauge faces and the schema gives no way to say.

### The needle's band is 3 percent, and the arithmetic says why

PLAN 4.6 asks for 1 percent. A measured bounding box carries up to one pixel of error per edge, so
two per axis. On the 550 pixel face that is 0.73 percent, inside 1 percent. On the 175 pixel needle
it is 2.31 percent, so a 1 percent band could fail a correct render. The face carries PLAN's 1
percent; the needle carries 3 percent with the arithmetic recorded in the fixture generator, and a
non-uniform scale still shows 9.09 percent.

## The review

DeepSeek reviewed the spec against the tree. Its first run spent all 60 tool calls reading and
produced no output, so it was resumed and asked to answer from what it had. It confirmed every fact
in the facts block against the files, found one imprecise wording (recorded as a correction in the
spec), and raised two findings that were already implemented by the time it answered, because it had
read the tree before the builders existed. Its hypothesis about the needle, that the slot anchor did
not match its alignment, was wrong: the anchor and alignment were correct and the cause was the
member sort. It said explicitly that it had not read `AnalogDialPrimitive.cpp` and was inferring,
which is the right way to report an unverified claim.

## What was not run, and why

- **No device run.** PLAN 4.6's gate is desktop captures.
- **Neither capture gate runs in CI and neither should.** GPU, driver and font rasterisation move
  these numbers and the hosted runners have no GPU. The dial gate measures extents rather than
  comparing to a reference, so it is less machine-specific than the chunk 11 gate, but it still
  needs a GPU to render at all.
- **ThreadSanitizer was not re-run.** Nothing here touches signal-core or any threaded path.
- **The history graph is not proven against a changing series.** `-udash-fraction` supplies a
  synthetic ramp, which proves the polyline renders, its extent, its auto scaling and its colour. A
  real series arrives with the connectors in PLAN 4.9, and only then does a changing trace mean
  anything.
- **The three gauges are not proven against a real value.** Same reason, same chunk.

## Known limitations

- **`-udash-fraction` is not data.** It supplies one synthetic constant to every bound signal so a
  gauge can be captured at a known deflection before a connector exists.
- **The sweep is fixed at 270 degrees from 225.** The schema has no field for it, and adding one was
  out of scope. A document cannot currently author a dial with a different sweep.
- **An `indicator` is still always drawn off**, carried over from chunk 11: no rule is evaluated
  yet, and lighting a lamp with no rule behind it would report a condition nothing measured.
- **`z_order` ties break by component id.** Deterministic, but two overlapping components that both
  omit `z_order` stack in an order the author did not choose.
